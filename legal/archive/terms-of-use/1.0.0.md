## 9. How orders work

1. **Placing an order or request.** When you place an order or purchase request, it is recorded as pending. This is a request to buy, not yet a confirmed sale.
2. **Acceptance.** For some requests, AgriBridge sends offers to eligible farmers, who may accept or decline within a set time. An order is **confirmed** only when the seller side has accepted and the order status moves to confirmed.
3. **Payment.** Payment status is tracked separately. A payment is treated as successful only after the payment provider's confirmation is verified by our server, not just because a page on your phone says so.
4. **Delivery.** An order then moves to in transit and delivered, or is cancelled.

Stock is reserved for confirmed orders. If we cannot match your request, we will tell you.
