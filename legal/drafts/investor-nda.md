# AgriBridge — Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (Investor Discussions)

**DRAFT TEMPLATE — not legal advice. Have a Ugandan advocate review before use.**

> **Important:** This is only a **confidentiality** agreement for early conversations. It is
> **not** an investment agreement and does **not** create any investment, share, or funding
> commitment. Any actual fundraising — term sheets, SAFEs, convertible notes, share
> subscription/shareholders' agreements, valuations — **must be prepared by a qualified
> lawyer** and may be regulated under Ugandan company and financial/securities law. Do not
> accept money from investors on the strength of this document alone.

---

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made on **[DATE]** between:

1. **[YOUR REGISTERED LEGAL NAME]**, of [ADDRESS], Uganda ("AgriBridge"); and
2. **[INVESTOR / COMPANY NAME]**, of [ADDRESS] ("Investor").

Each may disclose confidential information to the other to explore a possible business or
investment relationship (the "Purpose"). Each is a "Disclosing Party" when sharing and a
"Receiving Party" when receiving.

## 1. Confidential Information
Any non-public information disclosed for the Purpose, whether written, oral, or visual,
including business plans, financials, user and farmer data, technology, code, strategy,
pricing, and roadmaps — whether or not marked "confidential".

## 2. Obligations
The Receiving Party will:
- use the Confidential Information **only** for the Purpose;
- keep it **secret** and protect it with at least reasonable care;
- disclose it only to its officers/advisers who need it for the Purpose and are bound by
  similar confidentiality; and
- not copy or share it beyond what the Purpose requires.

## 3. Exclusions
Information that: is or becomes public without breach; was already lawfully known; is
independently developed without using the Confidential Information; or is lawfully received
from a third party without restriction.

## 4. Required disclosure
If law or a court compels disclosure, the Receiving Party will give prompt notice (where
lawful) and disclose only what is required.

## 5. Personal data
Any personal data shared (e.g., about farmers or users) must be handled in line with the
**Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019** and used only for the Purpose.

## 6. No licence, no obligation
Nothing here transfers any intellectual property or obligates either party to invest, buy,
sell, or enter any further agreement. All IP remains with its owner.

## 7. Return/destruction
On request or when discussions end, the Receiving Party will return or destroy the
Confidential Information (subject to records the law requires it to keep).

## 8. Term
Confidentiality obligations last for **[e.g., 3] years** from the date of disclosure.

## 9. No warranty
Confidential Information is provided "as is"; neither party warrants its accuracy for the
other's decisions.

## 10. Governing law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **Republic of Uganda**; disputes are subject to
the courts of Uganda at [CITY].
