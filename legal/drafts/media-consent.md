# AgriBridge — Media & Listing Consent

**DRAFT TEMPLATE — not legal advice. Have a Ugandan advocate review before use.**

This consent covers photos, videos, and listing details that Sellers submit to AgriBridge —
whether uploaded in the app or sent to us (e.g., by WhatsApp) for us to post on their behalf.

## Consent statement (to be accepted by the Seller)

> I, **[SELLER NAME]**, phone **[PHONE]**, confirm that:
>
> 1. The photos, videos, and information I provide are **mine to share**, are **true**, and
>    show the actual goods I am offering for sale.
> 2. I **grant AgriBridge a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence** to store, display, and
>    publish this media and my listing details on the AgriBridge Platform (web, app, USSD)
>    and in AgriBridge's own promotion of the marketplace, for as long as my listing is active
>    or until I ask AgriBridge to remove it.
> 3. Any people who appear in the media have **agreed** to appear.
> 4. I understand my **name, district, and contact details** may be shown to buyers so they
>    can reach me about the goods.
> 5. I can ask AgriBridge to **remove or update** my media/listing at any time by contacting
>    [EMAIL] / +256 [PHONE].
> 6. I will not upload content that is unlawful, misleading, or that I do not have the right
>    to share. AgriBridge may remove content that breaks these rules or its Terms of Use.
>
> **Accepted by:** [SELLER NAME]  **Date:** [DATE]
> (In the app, ticking the consent box when posting a listing is your acceptance.)

## How AgriBridge records this
- **In-app listings:** a consent checkbox on the listing form captures acceptance.
- **Media a Seller sends us to post for them (e.g., WhatsApp):** keep the chat/consent
  message on file, and/or have the Seller reply "I agree" to the statement above before
  publishing. Store the record with the listing.

## Removal
On request, AgriBridge will remove the Seller's media and delist the item within a reasonable
time, subject to any records the law requires us to keep.
