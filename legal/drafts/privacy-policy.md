## 1. Scope
This explains what personal data AgriBridge collects, why, how we protect it, and your rights.
It applies to the website, app, and USSD service.

## 2. Data we collect
- **You give us:** name, phone number, email, district/location, role (farmer/buyer/vendor),
  password (stored encrypted), listings, photos/videos you upload, orders, delivery addresses,
  reviews, and messages you send us.
- **Automatically:** basic usage/device information needed to run and secure the service.
- **Payments:** if you pay online, the payment provider processes your payment details; we
  receive a confirmation and reference, **not** your full card/mobile-money credentials.

## 3. Why we use it (lawful basis)
- **To provide the service** (create your account, publish listings, process orders, arrange
  delivery, show ratings) — performance of a contract with you.
- **With your consent** — to send you confirmations/updates by SMS or email, and to publish
  media you submit.
- **Legitimate interests** — safety, fraud prevention, improving the Platform.
- **Legal obligation** — where the law requires us to keep or disclose records.

## 4. Sharing
We share personal data only as needed to run the service:
- **Between Buyer and Seller** for a transaction (e.g., contact and delivery details).
- **Service providers** who help us operate (hosting/database, SMS, email, payment
  processing), under confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal/authorities** where required by Ugandan law.
We **do not sell** your personal data.

## 5. Your consent
By creating an account and ticking the consent box, you **consent** to AgriBridge collecting
and processing your personal data as described here, including sending you transactional SMS
and email. **You can withdraw consent** at any time (see Rights); this will not affect
processing already done, and some data may be retained where the law requires.

## 6. Your rights (Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019)
You may: access your data; correct inaccurate data; request deletion; object to or restrict
certain processing; and withdraw consent. To exercise a right, contact [EMAIL]. We aim to
respond within [e.g., 30] days. You may also complain to Uganda's **Personal Data Protection
Office (PDPO)**.

## 7. Security
We use technical and organisational measures — encrypted connections, access controls, and
database row-level security so users can only see data they are entitled to. No system is
perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

## 8. Retention
We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law,
then delete or anonymise it.

## 9. Children
The Platform is not intended for anyone under 18.

## 10. International transfers
Some service providers may process data outside Uganda. Where they do, we take steps
consistent with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 to protect your data.
