AgriBridge Privacy Policy and Data Protection Notice

Version 1.0.0 · Effective 9 October 2026

This notice explains, in plain language, what personal information AgriBridge collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and what rights you have. It applies to the website https://agribrige.com, the AgriBridge mobile app, and the AgriBridge USSD and SMS services.

Status of this document. This policy has been written for AgriBridge's current features. It has not yet been reviewed by a Ugandan advocate. Points marked "to be confirmed" are being finalised, and we will publish a new version and ask you to review it if anything material changes.

1. Who we are and what this covers

AgriBridge is an agricultural marketplace in Uganda that connects farmers, buyers, hotels and restaurants, and farm-input suppliers. We use the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 and its Regulations as our legal framework.

2. Who is responsible for your data (data controller)

AgriBridge, operating https://agribrige.com, decides how and why your personal data is used. Our registered legal name, company registration number, physical address and the name of our data protection or privacy officer have not yet been published. Until they are, all privacy matters go to the official contact below.

3. What personal data we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number, email address, district, and the account type you choose (farmer, buyer, hotel or restaurant, or input supplier).
  • Sign-in details: your login email or phone-based login. We do not store your password ourselves; it is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase).
  • Orders and transactions: what you ordered or sold, quantities, prices in UGX, delivery address or location, order status, and payment references.
  • Listings and media: produce, livestock or input listings, descriptions, photos and videos you upload.
  • Messages and support: messages you send us and questions you ask our AI assistant or crop doctor.
  • Records of your choices: the document versions you accepted, and your marketing preferences.

4. Information from farmers and sellers

If you sell, we also hold your listings, stock quantities, prices, district, verification status, and information needed to pay you or contact you about orders. Some details are shown to buyers (for example, your listing and district). Your phone number and exact location are only shared where needed to complete an order, as explained in section 11.

5. Information from buyers

If you buy, we hold your orders, delivery details, and contact details needed to arrange delivery or collection. For business buyers (hotels, restaurants) we may also hold the business name and invoicing details you give us.

6. Delivery partners and vendors

AgriBridge does not currently run a separate delivery-partner sign-up. If a delivery handler is involved in your order, we share only the delivery details they need for that order.

7. Technical information, cookies and usage data

We do not use advertising or analytics trackers. Our website stores small items in your browser (local storage) to keep you signed in, remember your basket, language, and cookie choice, and protect against repeated failed logins. Our servers keep technical logs (such as time, request type and error details) to keep the service secure and working. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, a script library from jsDelivr, and some images from Unsplash, so those providers may receive your IP address when your browser fetches them.

8. Why we use your data

  • To create and run your account, publish listings, and process orders and payments.
  • To match purchase requests with suitable farmers and send offers.
  • To send service messages about your account and orders.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents, and to resolve disputes.
  • To improve and fix the service.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting duties.
  • To send optional promotions, only if you separately agree (section 18).

9. Legal bases we rely on

  • Performing our agreement with you (account, listings, orders, payments, delivery).
  • Legal obligations (accounting, tax and lawful requests from authorities).
  • Legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, fixing and improving the service), balanced against your rights.
  • Your consent, where it is the right basis. Today that applies to optional promotional messages, and may apply to publishing your photos or videos beyond the listing itself. Consent is asked for separately. Accepting the Terms of Use or acknowledging this policy is not consent to promotional messages.

10. Order notifications and business alerts

When an order or purchase request is created, AgriBridge administrators are alerted so it can be handled. Eligible farmers receive an offer to fulfil it. Buyers and sellers receive updates about their own orders. These are service messages and are sent even if you opt out of promotions. Alert previews are kept short and do not include payment or full personal details.

11. Who your data is shared with in a transaction

  • Buyer and seller: to complete an order we share what each side needs, such as the product, quantity, delivery area and, where needed, a contact number. We aim to keep buyer and seller contact details private from each other until an order requires it. Only AgriBridge administrators can see both sides of a transaction.
  • AgriBridge administrators and staff see the order information they need to handle orders, support and disputes.

12. Service providers who process data for us

We use these providers, and only for the purpose shown. Some are optional and used only when configured:

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and file storage.
  • Render and Cloudflare — hosting and delivery of the website and API.
  • Africa's Talking — SMS and USSD.
  • Payment providers (for example Flutterwave, Pesapal, MTN Mobile Money) — payments, when enabled.
  • Google (Gemini) — AI assistant and crop-doctor answers. Do not put sensitive information in these questions.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging, Discord and Sentry — alerts and error monitoring for AgriBridge administrators. These may include limited technical or order identifiers, not payment credentials.

We do not sell your personal data.

13. Payments and transaction references

When you pay through a payment provider, the provider handles your card or mobile money credentials. We receive a confirmation, the amount, and a payment reference. We do not receive your full card number or mobile money PIN. We keep transaction records for accounting and dispute purposes.

14. Transfers outside Uganda

Our providers may store or process data in other countries. Where that happens, we ask providers to protect data in line with the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019. The exact hosting regions are to be confirmed.

15. Security

We use HTTPS for connections to the site and API, restrict administrator access, keep secrets out of the app code, and are putting in place logging of sensitive administrator actions. No system is perfectly secure. If a security incident affects your personal data, we will take steps to contain it and will notify you and the regulator where the law requires.

16. How long we keep data, and account deletion

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires. Order and payment records are kept for accounting, tax and dispute purposes. Exact retention periods for each type of data are to be confirmed with legal advice.

You can ask us to delete your account from your account settings. The current process removes or replaces your personal identifiers on your profile and account data. We keep order and payment records, in a form that does not identify you directly where possible, because we may need them for legal, tax, fraud or dispute reasons. Requests can need manual review if you have open orders or payments. We will tell you if we cannot complete a deletion and why.

17. Your rights and how to use them

Under the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 you may ask to see your data, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict some uses, and withdraw consent. You can use the privacy section in your account settings, or contact us:

  • Email: agribrige@gmail.com
  • Phone or WhatsApp: +256 755 966 690 (WhatsApp)

We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within the time allowed by law, which is to be confirmed with a Ugandan advocate. You can use these contact details without signing in.

18. Marketing choices

Promotional messages are optional and off by default. Today the only promotional channel we offer is SMS. You can choose it at sign-up or later in your account settings, and withdraw at any time there or by contacting us. Withdrawing does not affect order, payment or account messages.

19. Children

AgriBridge is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

20. Other websites and services

Links to other websites, payment pages or social media are not controlled by us. Please read their privacy notices.

21. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we publish a new version with an effective date and keep the older versions. If a change is material, we will ask you to review it before you continue using protected features.

22. Official contact

  • Business: AgriBridge
  • Website: https://agribrige.com
  • Email and support: agribrige@gmail.com
  • Phone and WhatsApp: +256 755 966 690

23. Complaints and the regulator

Please contact us first so we can try to fix the problem. You also have the right to complain to Uganda's Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO), which is part of the National Information Technology Authority, Uganda (NITA-U). Registration, complaint procedures and time limits under the Act are to be confirmed with a Ugandan advocate.
