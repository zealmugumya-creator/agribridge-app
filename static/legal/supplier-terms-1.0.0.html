AgriBridge Supplier Terms

Version 1.0.0 · Effective 9 October 2026

These terms apply in addition to the AgriBridge Terms of Use if you register as an input supplier (for example seed, fertiliser, tools, feed or veterinary products). They have not yet been reviewed by a Ugandan advocate.

1. Business details and verification

Give accurate business details. We may ask for proof that you are a genuine business, such as trading or registration documents, and for proof of the licences or permits needed for what you sell. We may approve, pause or suspend a supplier account while we check. What counts as sufficient verification is to be confirmed.

2. Listings, prices and stock

List only products you are entitled to sell, with correct names, brands, sizes, expiry dates where relevant, and prices in UGX. Keep stock and prices current and do not accept orders you cannot supply.

3. Regulated products

Agrochemicals, veterinary medicines and similar inputs are regulated in Uganda. You must hold the approvals the law requires, label products correctly, and follow safe-handling and storage rules. We may remove listings that appear unlawful or unsafe.

4. Fulfilment

Hand over or deliver goods as agreed, in the condition described, with the right documents or labels. Keep the buyer informed about delays.

5. Fees

AgriBridge may charge a service fee or commission, which will be shown to you before you accept an order.

6. Returns, complaints and refunds

If goods are faulty, expired, wrong or not delivered, you may be asked to replace or refund them. The order records will be used to decide what is fair, and you can give your side. Nothing here limits any right under the law.
